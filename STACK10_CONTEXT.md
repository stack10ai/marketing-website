## The Problem We Solve

Traditional consulting is broken. Legacy firms have monetised complexity — selling theoretical roadmaps, static 200-page decks, and multi-year "Digital Transformations" designed to maximise billable hours, not business value.

The result: Billions spent on strategy, yet recommended initiatives fail to be implemented because they are disconnected from operational and technical reality.

Worse: when AI solutions are built on broken processes, they simply automate dysfunction at scale.

**Stack10 replaces the era of "Advisory" with "Execution and Validation."** Transformation must be measured in working code deployed on optimised processes — not slides presented.

## Our Approach — The 5-Stage Model

Stack10 operates in partnership with **Keystone Intelligence** (process mining and validation specialists). Together they cover the full transformation cycle:

| Stage | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Identify** | Stack10 + Keystone | Operational/technical assessment + process mining to surface high-value AI use cases mapped to data-proven inefficiencies |
| **2. Architect** | Stack10 | Technical architecture, solution design, and ROI-linked development backlog |
| **3. Execute** | Stack10 | Build and deploy working AI solutions in production, integrated with existing systems |
| **4. Validate** | Keystone | Process mining post-deployment — adoption monitoring, impact quantification, ROI validation, unintended consequence detection |
| **5. Optimise & Scale** | Both | Continuous performance benchmarking, variant analysis, scaling playbook, early-warning system for AI degradation |

**One-month process intelligence platform deployment.**

- **Outcomes:** AS-IS process map from actual system data, bottleneck analysis, working AI prototype, prioritised opportunity register with ROI projections, technical architecture and backlog, executive briefing
- **Best for:** COOs, VPs of Operations, or Process Excellence leaders who want data-driven AI solutions with quantified ROI — not another PowerPoint

### Stack One — $50,000/month (Subscription)
**Monthly AI engineering subscription priced on story point output, not headcount.**  
Stack10 staffs the pod to deliver — senior AI engineers, architects, and QA as needed. Full IP transfer. Scale by increasing monthly commitment.

- **Outcomes:** 500 guaranteed story points per year, production AI systems deployed, validated ROI, full IP transfer, Solutions Architect and QA support
- **Best for:** CTOs or Heads of Engineering who have validated AI opportunities but lack in-house AI engineering talent to execute
