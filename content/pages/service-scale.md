## Section: The Adoption Problem

**Label:** Sound familiar?

**Heading:** The adoption gap hiding inside your AI investment

**Description:** If any of these are true, the issue isn't the tools. It's adoption.

### Pain cards

**01 — The tools are deployed. The habits aren't.**
You rolled out Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, or a custom AI tool six months ago. Uptake was high in week one. By week six, your team had reverted to the way they always worked. The licences are running. The workflows aren't.

**02 — Leadership and operations are on different planets.**
Your executives are enthusiastic about AI in strategy sessions. Your operations teams see it as extra work, a compliance risk, or just noise. Without a shared language and governance model, the gap widens — and nothing embeds.

**03 — The board is asking what changed.**
You've spent on AI. Now you need to show something for it. "We ran training sessions" isn't an answer. You need adoption rates, productivity delta, governance compliance — data that holds up in a board pack, not anecdotes from a workshop.

## Section: The 30/60/90 Measurement Framework

**Label:** The accountability framework

**Heading:** We don't measure training completion. We measure what changed.

**Description:** Anyone can hand your team a certificate. We give you a structured 90-day measurement cadence that tracks real behavioural change — and intervenes when it's not happening fast enough.

### Day 30 — Early adoption signals

**Body:** Usage data, workflow integration rates, and early champion activity are reviewed against baseline. We identify the quick-win gaps and act immediately — not at the end.

**Metrics measured:**
- Tool usage rates vs. pre-program baseline
- Champion activation by department
- Early friction points and blockers

### Day 60 — Embedding and adjustment

**Body:** Role-specific adoption gaps are addressed. Champion activity is benchmarked against target. Playbooks are refined based on what's actually working inside your organisation — not what worked somewhere else.

**Metrics measured:**
- Role-specific adoption gap analysis
- Playbook refinement and redeployment
- Champion performance benchmarking

### Day 90 — Validated outcomes

**Body:** Full program review against the baseline metrics set on day one. Adoption rates, productivity delta, and governance compliance are reported with data — ready for your board, not dressed up for it.

**Metrics measured:**
- Adoption rate vs. target
- Productivity delta by role and department
- Governance compliance status
- Board-ready outcome report

### Callout quote

The difference between Stack Scale and a training vendor: at day 90 you have a data set, not a participant list.
