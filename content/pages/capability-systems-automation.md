## Section: What We Build

**Label:** What we build

**Heading:** Six types of automation. One standard: production-ready.

**Description:** Not scripts. Not prototypes. Autonomous systems designed to handle real business volume, with the error handling, observability, and compliance trails your environment requires.

### Cards

**1. Agentic Systems**
AI agents that perceive, reason, plan, and execute multi-step tasks autonomously — with human-in-the-loop gates where compliance or risk requires them. Designed for complex workflows, not just simple triggers.

**2. Enterprise Integrations**
Production-grade connectors for SAP, Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, and legacy systems. Data flows between platforms reliably, bidirectionally, and observably — without breaking every quarter.

**3. Process Automation**
End-to-end automation of high-volume, rules-heavy processes: compliance submissions, approval workflows, reconciliation, onboarding, invoicing — the repetitive work that consumes your team's time.

**4. Compliance Automation**
Automated audit trails, regulatory reporting, and decision logging for APRA CPS 230, AML/CTF, and internal governance frameworks — so compliance is a by-product of operations, not a separate workstream.

**5. Workflow Orchestration**
Complex multi-step pipelines with conditional routing, error handling, retry logic, and human escalation paths — not just a linear script that breaks the moment something unexpected happens.

**6. Legacy System Modernisation**
AI and automation layers built on top of systems you cannot replace — so you get modern capability without a multi-year re-platform project. The existing system stays. The manual work goes.
