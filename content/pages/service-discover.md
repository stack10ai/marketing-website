### Approach 1: Interview & Survey-Led

**Context:** Best for organisations at the start of their AI journey, or when you need a board-ready strategy without waiting for system integrations. Fast to mobilise.

**How we work:**
- Executive and stakeholder interviews across departments
- Process walkthroughs and workflow documentation
- Survey-based opportunity scanning across the organisation
- ROI modelling from operational data gathered through interviews and process walkthroughs
- Effort vs impact scoring across all identified opportunities — ranked on a 5-point scale so the highest-impact, lowest-effort items surface first

**What you receive:**
- Prioritised AI opportunity backlog — scored by impact and effort, ranked so the best opportunities (high impact, low effort) are actioned first
- ROI model mapping each opportunity to measurable outcomes
- Strategic AI recommendations with rationale
- Board-ready presentation and business case

**Scope note:** Deliverable depth scales with your organisation — from targeted backlog to full strategic roadmap.

---

### Approach 2: Process Mining & Live Data *(featured)*

**Context:** Best for regulated environments, complex high-volume operations, or when you need evidence — not opinions — to defend your strategy. We connect to your live systems.

**How we work:**
- Live system integration — ERP, CRM, HRIS, and operational platforms
- Process mining — what's actually happening, not what people say is happening
- Bottleneck identification and inefficiency quantification from real data
- Technology and data readiness assessment against your current stack
- Effort vs impact scoring across all identified opportunities — ranked on a 5-point scale so the highest-impact, lowest-effort items surface first

**What you receive:**
- Data-driven process maps drawn from your live systems
- Bottleneck and inefficiency analysis with quantified impact
- Prioritised AI opportunity backlog — scored by impact and effort, ranked so the best opportunities (high impact, low effort) are actioned first
- Quantified ROI projections grounded in your actual operational data
- Process intelligence analysis — specialist consultants implement a process intelligence tool embedded in the engagement, so every recommendation is grounded in your actual system data

**Scope note:** Platform scope and integration depth scaled to your environment. Proposal scoped after initial conversation.
