60% Faster Document Processing — Government Agency

How a federal government agency modernised document processing with AI, achieving 60% faster processing while maintaining 94% accuracy.

60%
Faster Processing
94%
Accuracy Maintained
12
Weeks to Production
The Challenge

This federal agency processes over 120,000 citizen-submitted documents per month across multiple programme areas. Each document requires classification, data extraction, validation against eligibility criteria, and routing to the appropriate assessment team.

The existing process was almost entirely manual. Processing times averaged 21 business days. A backlog of 35,000 documents was growing by 2,000 per month. Staff morale was declining as overtime increased.

The Approach

Phase 1: Process Mining (Stack Map, 4 weeks)

We started with Stack Map, deployed by Keystone Intelligence. The team extracted event logs from the case management system covering 18 months of document processing activity — 2.1 million individual events across 1.4 million documents.

Process mining revealed:

  • 31 distinct processing variants existed (staff believed there were 6)
  • Document classification was the biggest bottleneck
  • 18% of documents were misclassified, triggering rework adding 8 days
  • Three programme areas accounted for 72% of volume

Phase 2: AI Prototype (Stack Audit, 6 weeks)

  • Automated Document Classification: A multi-modal AI model trained on 500,000 historically classified documents
  • Intelligent Data Extraction: An NLP system that extracts key fields from citizen documents

Both systems designed with mandatory human review for edge cases.

Phase 3: Production Deployment (Stack One, 2 weeks)

Deployed to sovereign Australian cloud environment (AWS Sydney) with full compliance documentation.

Results
  • 60% reduction in average processing time (21 days → 8.4 days)
  • 94% classification accuracy (vs 82% manual baseline)
  • Document backlog cleared within 6 weeks of deployment
  • 35% reduction in staff overtime hours
"Stack10 showed us the real problem with data, not opinions. The process mining findings alone were worth the investment — the AI system was the bonus that transformed our operations."
— Director of Digital Services, Federal Government Agency
