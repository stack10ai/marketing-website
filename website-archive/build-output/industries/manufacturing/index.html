The Challenges

Your factory runs on systems that don't talk to each other

SCADA on the floor, SAP in the office, spreadsheets in between. Here's what keeps manufacturing leaders up at night.

SAP ECC migration cliff

End of support in 2027 means a forced migration to S/4HANA — but your OT integrations, custom ABAP, and plant-floor workflows don't have a migration plan yet.

OT/IT convergence gap

SCADA/PLC systems are air-gapped for safety. Getting sensor data into analytics platforms without compromising OT security is a design problem, not a settings change.

Industry 4.0 pilot graveyard

Your IoT proof-of-concept worked. But scaling from one line to the whole plant stalls on data quality, change management, and integration debt.

ISSB sustainability deadline

ISSB reporting kicks in July 2026. Energy consumption, Scope 1–3 emissions, and waste data are scattered across manual processes and disconnected systems.

Use Case 01

Predictive Maintenance

Connect vibration, temperature, and pressure sensor data from SCADA/PLC systems to ML models that predict failures before they cause unplanned downtime.

IoT Sensors →Data Pipeline →Failure Prediction →Work Order
