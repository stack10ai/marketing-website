Healthcare & Health Insurance

Your hospital runs on 20-year-old systems. Your patients expect 2026.

Legacy HIS platforms, manual prior auth, paper-based clinical coding — Australian healthcare is drowning in operational debt. Stack10 builds production AI systems that ship in weeks, not years.

The Challenge

Healthcare IT is held together with duct tape

Legacy HIS/EMR lock-in

Epic, Cerner, DXC — your core systems are decades old. Every integration is a custom project. Every upgrade is a multi-year saga.

Cybersecurity at board level

The Medibank breach changed everything. Patient data protection is now a board-level concern, but your security posture can't keep up with the threat landscape.

Prior auth takes days, not minutes

Manual workflows, fax-based approvals, and disconnected systems mean patients wait days for treatment authorisation that should take minutes.

HL7 FHIR & My Health Record

Interoperability mandates are here. My Health Record integration isn't optional. But your integration engine is already overloaded.

Use Cases

AI that ships in weeks, not years

Three high-ROI applications we build for healthcare organisations — validated with process mining before a line of code is written.

Use Case 01

Claims Processing & Prior Auth Automation

Process

Manual adjudication, fax-based prior auth, multi-day turnaround with 25% denial rates

AI

Intelligent document extraction, automated eligibility checks, real-time adjudication engine

Outcome

Prior auth in minutes not days. 60% reduction in claims processing cost. Denial rate halved.

Use Case 02

Patient Flow Optimisation

Process

ED triage bottlenecks, manual bed management, delayed discharges creating capacity crises

AI

Predictive bed demand, automated discharge planning triggers, real-time flow dashboards

Outcome

20% improvement in ED throughput. Bed occupancy optimised. Patient experience transformed.

Use Case 03

Clinical Coding Automation

Process

Manual ICD-10/DRG coding from discharge summaries. Backlogs, errors, and revenue leakage.

AI

NLP-driven code suggestion from clinical notes, automated DRG assignment with coder review

Outcome

95%+ coding accuracy. 70% reduction in coder workload. Revenue uplift from correct DRG capture.
