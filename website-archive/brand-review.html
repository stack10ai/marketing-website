USAGE RULES
Website
Core palette only: Black, White, Green scale, Grey scale, Cream scale. No mint or lime on the website.
Social Media
Core palette + Mint, Lime, Sand for CTA banners and social media. Always paired with black text.
Green Text Rule
Brand green (#6CE47C) for dark backgrounds and large decorative numbers only. Use --green-900 (#1A7A2E) for text on light backgrounds (WCAG 4.5:1).
