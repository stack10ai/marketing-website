The Reality

Mid-market financial services is under siege

Regulatory deadlines are live, legacy systems are choking innovation, and AI experiments aren't making it to production.

CPS 230 is live — gaps remain

APRA's operational resilience standard took effect July 2025. Most mid-tier institutions still lack mapped critical processes and third-party risk visibility across 20-30 providers.

Legacy systems eat the budget

Core banking platforms like Ultracs, FIS Profile, and Temenos T24 consume 60-70% of IT spend — leaving almost nothing for innovation while Big 4 banks spend $8.9B combined.

AI pilots stall at proof-of-concept

95% of AI projects in financial services never reach production. Without understanding actual process flows, automation targets the wrong problems.

AML/CTF reform is next

March 2026 brings outcomes-based AML/CTF obligations. Rules-based transaction monitoring won't cut it — AUSTRAC wants demonstrated effectiveness, not checkbox compliance.

Cyber threats are escalating

Post super fund breaches, cybersecurity is board-level. CPS 234 alignment and operational resilience aren't optional — they're survival requirements.

Neobanks are stealing share

Loan origination cycles of 15-30 days are losing borrowers to fintechs. Digital-native competitors move in hours, not weeks.

Use Cases

AI that ships in financial services

Three high-impact use cases we deliver for Australian mid-market institutions.

01

Claims Processing Automation Insurers

ASIC is scrutinising claims handling speed. We mine your FNOL-to-settlement process, identify bottlenecks and manual handoffs, then deploy AI to triage, assess, and fast-track straightforward claims — reducing cycle time while improving consistency.

Process Mining → AI Triage Model → STP Automation → ↓ Cycle Time
02

AML/CTF Monitoring Optimisation Banks · Credit Unions

With outcomes-based obligations arriving March 2026, rules-based transaction monitoring generates excessive false positives and can't demonstrate effectiveness. We deploy process intelligence to optimise alert triage, then build AI models that reduce false positives while improving detection rates — giving you demonstrable outcomes for AUSTRAC.

Alert Flow Analysis → ML Detection Model → ↓ False Positives → Outcome Evidence
03

Loan Origination Process Mining Banks · Mutuals

Application-to-settlement cycles of 15-30 days are losing borrowers to fintechs and neobanks. We map the actual origination process end-to-end, expose rework loops and approval bottlenecks, then automate document verification and decisioning to compress turnaround dramatically.

End-to-End Mapping → Bottleneck Analysis → AI Decisioning → ↓ Days to Settle
